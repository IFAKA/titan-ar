Convention
World: X right, Y up, Z toward the viewer, metres. A tag stands vertical on a wall/screen, face toward +Z. Optional per tag: yaw_deg (about Y), pitch_deg (about X; -90 = flat on floor, face up), roll_deg, size_mm. size_mm = black square edge (ruler). Camera yaw 0 = looking toward -Z, + = turn left; pitch + = up; roll + = right side down. Lens distortion is not corrected (calibration is the next step).
