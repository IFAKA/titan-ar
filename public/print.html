Print at 100% (actual size, NOT "fit to page"). Use matte paper if you can. After printing, measure the 100 mm bar on each sheet with a ruler: it must be 100 mm. Then measure the black square and tell the size to the tag map. Total sheet width = black square x 1.25 (it has a white border that must stay white); max black square for A4 is about 160 mm.
