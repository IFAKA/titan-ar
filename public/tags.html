Lower the screen brightness (about 30-50%) and avoid reflections: a glossy bright screen washes out the black/white squares and the phone camera loses the corners. Screen sizes differ: use a ruler to measure the BLACK square (not the white border) on the glass and type its edge in mm above. Put the same number in the tag map (size_mm) on the phone page.
